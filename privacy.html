Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

TOEFLPrep is a free practice website focused on TOEFL Complete the Words practice. We aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

TOEFLPrep does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, password, date of birth, or payment information. Practice answers and timer state are processed in your browser for the purpose of running a session.
